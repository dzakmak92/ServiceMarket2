## What changed from the hunt
- **I-03 rose to the top.** Trades are asking verbatim for the fee-on-award model, and Angi's own app ratings favour it. It is also the founder's existing product.
- **I-04 dropped.** heidisystems already sells the full HeizKlar concept, so differentiation is only 2.
- **I-01, I-06 and I-08 dropped.** Direct competitors already sell the exact concept (Mangomint, Elterntakt), or the platform owner gates access (ServiceTitan).
- **I-09 cannot stand alone.** E-invoice receiving is free or bundled in tools with 14k+ ratings.
