# Project Idea (Parenting DACH hunt): Einspringer

Generated by `h:wedge` from winning topic **R-01** (see `docs/blueprint-parenting/01-discovery.md`).

## The idea
- **Working name:** Einspringer
- **One sentence:** Employer-paid backup childcare for small and mid-sized firms. When a child is sick or the Kita is closed, an employee books a vetted carer who comes home within hours, and the employer pays through a yearly plan using the €600 tax-free allowance.
- **Why now:**
  - sick-child days drop from 15 to 10 per child and parent on 1.1.2027
  - § 3 Nr. 34a b EStG allows €600 per employee per year tax-free
  - the skills shortage makes every lost day costly
  - KMU are underserved by corporate family services (pme)

## The user
- **Buyer:** owners and HR of KMU with 10–250 staff (Handwerk, services, practices, agencies) in one German metro first.
- **User:** employees with children aged 0–12.
- **Problem today:** no backup when a child is sick or the Kita closes. Parents take unpaid leave or call in sick, and the employer loses the day.
- **How they solve it today:** grandparents, partners swapping, unpaid leave; corporates use pme or Notfallmamas contracts; babysitter sites (Betreut 1.3★ on Trustpilot).
- **Evidence:** R-01, scan/P7.md (25 signals); WTP 5 (employer contracts); kinderkrankengeld 6,600 and kinderkrankentage 5,400 searches/mo `[E]`.

## The product
- **Must do on day one:**
  - employer account and plan with eligible employees
  - parent booking: date, time, child's age, sick or closure, address
  - matching to available vetted carers (radius, availability, rating) with confirmation and ETA
  - carer profile (certificates: extended Führungszeugnis, first aid for children, insurance)
  - check-in/check-out, hours log and in-app messaging
  - monthly employer invoice with a § 3 Nr. 34a b-ready statement
- **Reuse from ServiceMarket:** bookings, live location/ETA, chat, ratings, Stripe, calendar.
- **Must NOT be:** nursing or medical care, a babysitter classifieds site, holiday camps, or an app for children.
- **Platform:** mobile-first web app (PWA) for parents and carers; web dashboard for employers
- **Languages:** German (du for parents and carers, Sie for employers [A])
- **Market:** one German metro first; AT/CH later (their tax rules differ)
- **Offline needed:** no
- **Multiple users share data:** yes (employer, parent, carer roles)

## Business
- **How it makes money:** €490 setup once plus a yearly plan (€15 per eligible parent per month or €1,490 flat for up to 15 parents, 8 h/parent/yr included) plus extra hours at €32/h
- **First 100 users:** 20 KMU conversations via ServiceMarket business contacts, IHK/HWK, LinkedIn; carers via student job boards and Tagesmütter networks
- **Unfair advantage:** an existing marketplace stack (booking, ETA, chat, payments) and a KMU/Handwerk network in DACH

## Constraints
- **Appetite:** 6 weeks [A]
- **Stack:** confirm in Phase 2 (reuse ServiceMarket: React + FastAPI + MongoDB)
- **Compliance:**
  - GDPR (children's data, address, health context: minimise)
  - carer legal status (employment vs freelance; Scheinselbstständigkeit and Arbeitnehmerüberlassung checks)
  - extended Führungszeugnis
  - liability insurance
  - tax treatment confirmed by a Steuerberater
