# Project Idea (DACH hunt): HeizKlar

Generated by `h:wedge` from winning topic **Q-01** (see `docs/blueprint-dach/01-discovery.md`). The first hunt's `PROJECT_IDEA.md` (ClaimClock) in the repo root is unchanged.

## The idea
- **Working name:** HeizKlar
- **One sentence:** For small landlords and Hausverwaltungen (2–30 units) in Germany, HeizKlar reads any standard radio heating meter, sends the legally required monthly consumption info (UVI) and produces the yearly heating-cost statement, as a third-party service whose costs are passed to tenants.
- **Why now:**
  - remote-readable meters are mandatory by 31.12.2026 (§5 HeizkostenV)
  - the monthly UVI duty applies (§6a), and tenants may cut 3% if it is missing (§12)
  - landlords are leaving objego after price hikes of up to 350%
  - searches are rising (heizkostenabrechnung 8,100/mo; trend at a 5-year high in Nov 2026) `[E]`

## The user
- **Who exactly:** private landlords with 2–30 units and small Hausverwaltungen. They are 40–70, use Excel, are moderately tech-savvy and price-sensitive.
- **Problem today:** they must comply with UVI and HeizkostenV/CO2KostAufG, and the default is a 10-year ista/Techem contract at €60–120 per unit per year, or manual work.
- **How they solve it today:** a Messdienst contract; Excel plus manual readings; vermieter1.de (PDFs from manual readings); hellohousing Pro (€234 per house per year).
- **Evidence:** Q-01, scan/D7.md (25 signals); WTP 4; volume and trend above `[E]`.

## The product
- **Must do on day one:**
  - house/unit and meter registry
  - import readings from an OMS/wM-Bus gateway (2 brands), with CSV or photo fallback
  - monthly UVI as PDF + e-mail and tenant link
  - yearly heating-cost statement (HeizkostenV split, CO2KostAufG landlord/tenant split) as a line item for the Nebenkostenabrechnung
  - invoice to the landlord as a third-party billing service
- **Must NOT be:** a full property-management suite, a meter manufacturer, or legal advice.
- **Platform:** web first (responsive)
- **Languages:** German (Sie)
- **Market:** Germany first; Austria (HeizKG) and Switzerland (cantonal VHKA) later
- **Offline needed:** no
- **Multiple users share data:** yes, later (HV staff, owners, tenant read-only links)

## Business
- **How it makes money:** a one-time onboarding (€149 per house, devices at cost, installer commission via ServiceMarket) plus recurring €2.90 per unit per month (passed through to tenants)
- **First 100 users:** deadline content in German, answers in landlord forums (Buhl/WISO, Haus & Grund local groups, r/Mietrecht, Facebook landlord groups), installers from the ServiceMarket network who meet landlords during retrofits
- **Unfair advantage:** a DACH trades marketplace with Heizung/Sanitär/Elektro installers, a pricing catalogue and invoicing toolkits

## Constraints
- **Appetite:** 6 weeks [A]
- **Stack:** confirm in Phase 2 (existing ServiceMarket stack: React + FastAPI + MongoDB)
- **Budget for services:** about €300 test hardware, plus a tenancy-law review of templates
- **Compliance:** GDPR (consumption data is personal data); HeizkostenV, CO2KostAufG, BetrKV; meter calibration (Eichrecht) stays with the device manufacturer
