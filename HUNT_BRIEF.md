# Hunt Brief

## You
- **Audiences you belong to or can reach:** trades and home-service businesses (built ServiceMarket, a MyHammer-style marketplace for AT/DE with 149 priced job types across 24 trades); homeowners and renters who hire tradespeople
- **Skills:** full-stack (React + Tailwind, FastAPI, MongoDB), Stripe billing, multilingual apps (EN/DE/TR/ES), GDPR / Austrian DSG compliance
- **Unfair advantage:** an existing pricing catalogue and pro toolkits (invoice, tax, project management) for trades; DACH market knowledge

## Where to hunt
- **Grounds to search:** B2B and B2C, SaaS and apps (see `docs/blueprint/01-discovery.md` → Hunting grounds)
- **Platforms you trust or use:** Reddit, App Store / Google Play reviews, Trustpilot, Google SERP (forums, G2/Capterra snippets), TikTok / YouTube comments
- **Seed pains you've noticed:** quoting and pricing jobs, chasing invoices, finding reliable tradespeople

## Rule-outs
- **Won't build:** medical, legal or financial advice; gambling; crypto; apps for children
- **Customer type:** any (B2B small businesses and B2C consumers / prosumers)

## Constraints
- **Platform preference:** any (web first is the default, given the existing stack)
- **Appetite:** 6 weeks for the MVP [A]
- **Hours per week:** (open)
- **Budget for services:** (open)
- **Market and languages:** USA, Europe (UK, DACH, EU), Australia; English first
- **Minimum price you'd charge:** 9 USD/month B2C, 29 USD/month B2B [A]
